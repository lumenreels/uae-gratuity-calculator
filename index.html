For expatriate employees in private-sector mainland jobs with a full-time contract. Enter your last basic salary (not housing, transport or other allowances).

How it is calculated

  • Less than 1 year of continuous service: no gratuity.
  • First 5 years: 21 days of basic wage per year. After that: 30 days per year. Part years count in proportion.
  • Daily wage here = monthly basic / 30. The total cannot exceed 2 years of wage (24 months of basic).
  • Unpaid absence days are not counted as service.
  • The employer can deduct amounts you owe, and must pay within 14 days of the end of the contract.

Source: u.ae, End of service benefits for workers in the private sector. Free zones (DIFC, ADGM), part-time contracts, and the MoHRE savings scheme follow different rules. This is an estimate, not legal advice. Check your contract and MoHRE if there is a dispute.
